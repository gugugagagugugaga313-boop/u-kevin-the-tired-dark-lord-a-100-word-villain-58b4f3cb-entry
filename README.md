## The monologue

Brine keeps the tide-gate that holds the sea out of the city — one turn
of the wheel a day, paid in salt and silence. His brother thought it
should be two turns. His brother is at the bottom of the harbour now.
A hero has come for the key, and Brine is happy to hand it over, with
one small condition attached.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the monologue — 101 words, 504 characters —
to `/output/entry.txt`. It is fully deterministic: no network, no
randomness, no clock. Two runs produce byte-identical output.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
